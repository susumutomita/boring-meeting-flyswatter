# Personas

## P1. Meeting Participant

The participant is in a recurring meeting and notices long pauses or disengagement. They want a lightweight way to surface the boredom moment without calling out another person.

### Needs

- Start and stop the app quickly.
- Avoid recording meeting content.
- Understand when boredom was detected.
- Finish the intervention quickly.

## P2. Meeting Facilitator

The facilitator wants to improve meeting quality after the meeting. They need structured feedback that is easy to discuss and not personally accusatory.

### Needs

- See first boredom timing.
- See reason tags.
- See subjective productivity score.
- Compare peer recap signals.

## P3. Hackathon Reviewer

The reviewer needs to evaluate whether the project has a clear intent, creative fit, working MVP, and coherent technical execution.

### Needs

- Understand the product within one minute.
- See a working browser demo.
- See that the concept is implemented, not just pitched.
- See AI-DLC documentation and Inception completion.

## P4. Privacy-Conscious User

The privacy-conscious user is willing to use a meeting companion only if it does not record audio or collect identifying free text.

### Needs

- No accounts.
- No audio recording.
- No transcription.
- Preset reasons only.
- Local-first operation.
